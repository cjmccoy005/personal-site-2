About

Hi, I’m CJ. My whole career has come back to one question, asked at different scales: what are we trying to build, why, and how will we get there?

Some days that means sitting with executives at a Fortune 100 company, clarifying growth strategy or reimagining ways of working. Other days it means partnering one-on-one to help someone get unstuck and back into the driver seat of their lives.

Both come down to vision, analysis, focus, and a set of clear next steps.

Background

Ten-plus years inside Fortune 100 organizations

Growth strategy, operating models, and the unglamorous work of getting large groups of people pointed the same direction.

A certified coach, not just a strategist

Credentialed through the International Coaching Federation (ACC). I coach individuals on careers and lives that feel more intentional, and the same skillset shapes how I partner with leaders and teams in large enterprises.

Currently leading AI enablement at a healthcare enterprise

Helping a several-hundred-person organization move from experimenting with new technology to genuinely working differently.

The work
