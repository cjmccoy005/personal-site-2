Career and life design coaching

Most people come to me at a pivot point. Something about the current path has stopped fitting, and the next step isn’t obvious yet. We start there.

How I work

I’m a certified coach, credentialed through the International Coaching Federation, and I also advise students and alumni through Brown’s Office of Career & Life Design.

Coaching, for me, is not advice-giving. It’s asking better questions than the ones you’ve been asking yourself, and staying with them long enough to get somewhere honest.

I bring the same discipline I use with executives: name where you’re actually trying to go, decide what matters most, then build a path you can walk on a normal week, not a perfect one.

Ways to work together

One-to-one coaching

Ongoing engagements for people navigating a career decision, a transition, or a quieter question about how they want to spend their working life.

Team & leadership workshops

Sessions for teams who need shared language for where they’re going and how they want to work, run with the same tools I use one-to-one.

Speaking

Talks on career and life design, intentional work, and what changes for people when technology changes their job.
